# Office Policy Guide — DOH–CHD CALABARZON Recruitment Portal

*A one-page guide for the office. No technical knowledge needed.*

The portal separates two kinds of rules:

- **Rules that never change** — the ones civil-service practice fixes. These
  are built into the system and no one needs to manage them.
- **Rules the office chooses** — the ones that reasonably differ between
  offices or over time. These live on one screen, the **Office Policy** page,
  where the HRM Chief can change them. Every change is recorded in the audit
  log with who changed it and when.

Nothing on this page requires a programmer. If office practice differs from a
setting below, the HRM Chief flips the switch and the system follows.

## The seven settings (and how they ship)

Open **Office Policy** from the sidebar (visible to the HRM Chief only).

1. **Extending a vacancy after the exam is scheduled — OFF.**
   Once an examination has been scheduled, the publication period can no
   longer be extended; late applicants would form a second cohort that never
   sat the same exam. If more applicants are needed, cancel and re-publish.
   *Turn ON only if the office genuinely runs second sittings.*

2. **Extending a document-resubmission deadline — ON.**
   When an applicant is given 2 weeks to fix a document and needs more time,
   the reviewer can extend the deadline. The extension requires a reason, is
   recorded, and the applicant is emailed the new date. Nothing ever forfeits
   automatically.

3. **Who decides an interview no-show — the HR support office.**
   When a Plantilla candidate misses the panel interview, the HR office that
   schedules interviews receives the letter of intent and decides reschedule
   or not-proceed. *Switch to "stage owner / panel" if the board itself should
   decide.*

4. **The owning office may record withdrawals — ON.**
   An applicant's withdrawal letter usually arrives at the HR office even
   while the case sits with the panel or the Director. With this ON, the
   owning office (Secretariat for Level 1, HRM Chief for Level 2) can record
   it at any stage before completion.

5. **When a selection can no longer be rescinded — once a contract or
   appointment reference is recorded.**
   Up to that point, a wrongly recorded selection can be rescinded with a
   reason (the candidate pool is restored and everyone affected is notified
   that the decision is under review). After the appointment papers exist, a
   revocation is a separation matter, outside recruitment. Rescinding also
   voids the appointee's completion checklist so nothing stale carries over.

6. **COS postings filling several positions — OFF.**
   A single-batch COS posting fills one position. *Turn ON to let a posting
   declare "positions to fill" (for example, three nurses on one posting),
   and its selection round will allow that many picks.*

7. **Suspend and Close require a reason — ON.**
   Pausing or closing a vacancy asks for a short reason, which lands in the
   audit trail next to the action.

## Decisions that are built in (no switch)

These reflect how the system protects the public record. Each can still be
changed later — it is a small, well-marked adjustment — but they are not
day-to-day settings:

- A **published closing date can never be moved earlier**. Corrections go
  through Suspend or Cancel, never a quiet shortening.
- A **cancelled vacancy stays cancelled**. Running the recruitment again means
  publishing a new vacancy with a new code.
- An applicant who **withdrew**, or whose **vacancy was cancelled**, may apply
  again; a rejected applicant may not.
- The office can **correct an applicant's email or phone** (with a reason);
  both the old and new address are notified.
- The Appointing Authority can **select past a withdrawal**, or record
  **"No appointment — failure of search"**, closing the remaining candidates
  properly instead of leaving the vacancy stuck.
- The board may **recommend no candidate** (with its reasons); any selection
  from such a report requires a written justification.
- The **Comparative Assessment Report** carries its deliberation date and the
  number of members present, and returning it to the board corrects only the
  ETE ratings and the recommendation — finalized exam and interview scores
  stay locked.
- Completion requirements record the **date the hard copy was actually
  received**, so "late" means late in fact.
- If a communicated result is ever reversed, the applicant receives a
  **neutral "decision under review" notice** — no one is left believing an
  outcome that no longer stands.
