{% comment %} SL-14 — Rescind a recorded final selection. A selection should be hard to change, but "impossible" turns a mis-click — or the selected person withdrawing before appointment — into the official truth forever. Rescinding never edits or deletes the original: it marks it rescinded, keeps every recorded value, and frees the vacancy so a replacement decision can be recorded as a new, linked record. Office Policy supplies the cutoff; the original selection and its correction trail stay intact.
