{% blocktranslate trimmed %}How to fill up your Personal Data Sheet (PDS){% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}The Personal Data Sheet (CS Form No. 212) is the standard government form that tells us who you are, where you studied, and what work you have done. Every applicant submits one. This page explains it in plain language and points out the mistakes we see most often. It follows the Civil Service Commission’s official guide — if anything here is ever unclear, the CSC guide is the final word.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Before you start{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}You will need:{% endblocktranslate %}

  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}A computer with Microsoft Excel. The PDS must be typed, not handwritten. Free programs such as LibreOffice can open the file, but they may break its tick boxes and page layout — check every page carefully if you use one. Filling it up on a phone is not practical; use a computer, or ask a computer shop for help.{% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Your school records, eligibility or license details, and job history — including exact dates.{% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}A recent passport-size photo (4.5 cm × 3.5 cm), taken within the last six months, with no filters. A digital photo is fine.{% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Any valid government ID.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Take your time. It is normal for this to take an hour or two the first time. Save your file as you go.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}The five rules that cause the most corrections{% endblocktranslate %}

  1. {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Write your name in this order: SURNAME, FIRST NAME, NAME EXTENSION, MIDDLE NAME. Name extension means Jr., Sr., III — leave it blank if you have none. Use the same order for your spouse and your parents.{% endblocktranslate %}
  2. {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Dates are day/month/year (dd/mm/yyyy). 5 March 2001 is 05/03/2001, not 03/05/2001.{% endblocktranslate %}
  3. {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Never leave a box empty. Write N/A if it does not apply to you. A blank box looks like a forgotten answer; N/A shows you read the question.{% endblocktranslate %}
  4. {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Do not abbreviate. Write the complete name of every school, office, and organization — Civil Service Commission, not CSC; BS Psychology, not BSP.{% endblocktranslate %}
  5. {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Answer everything truthfully. The form carries a legal warning: a false statement in the PDS can be grounds for an administrative or criminal case.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Section by section{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Your full name, birth date and birthplace, sex, civil status, height, weight, citizenship, addresses, and contact details. If your civil status is “Others”, say what it is. If you hold dual or foreign citizenship, pick the country from the list in the form. “Agency employee number” means your employee ID in the office where you work now — write N/A if you are not a government employee.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Your spouse, your parents, and all your children with their birth dates. Two things people miss:{% endblocktranslate %}

  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Your mother’s name here is her maiden name — her name before she married (pangalan bago ikasal).{% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}List every child, even those who are adults or no longer living with you.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}For each level — elementary, secondary, vocational, college, graduate studies:{% endblocktranslate %}

  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}The full name of the school.{% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}The course, written in full.{% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}The school years you attended, like 2015-2019.{% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}If you finished, the year you graduated. If you did not finish, write the highest grade, level, or number of units you earned — that is a valid answer, not a blank.{% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Any scholarship or academic honor you received.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}For elementary, write PRIMARY EDUCATION if you graduated. For high school, write HIGH SCHOOL if you finished under the old curriculum, or JUNIOR HIGH SCHOOL / SENIOR HIGH SCHOOL under K-12.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Every eligibility you hold, with your rating, and the date and place of the examination or conferment. This includes eligibilities granted by law, not only exams — honor graduate, barangay official, veteran preference, and similar. If your eligibility is a professional license (RA 1080), add the license number and its expiry date.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Write N/A if you have none. Not having an eligibility does not disqualify you from every position.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}All your jobs — government and private — starting with your current or most recent one. For each: exact start and end dates, the complete position title, the complete name of the office or company, your monthly salary in figures (for example P21,877.00), salary grade and step increment if you had one (written like 24-2), your employment status (permanent, temporary, casual, contractual), and whether it was government service (yes or no).{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Important for job applicants: the CSC also asks for a separate Work Experience Sheet (WES) when you apply for a vacant position. It lists only the experience relevant to the job you are applying for, and it is submitted together with your PDS. You can download the blank WES at the top of this page.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Any volunteer work with an organization — the organization’s complete name and address, your dates, the number of hours, and what you did there. Barangay, church, school, and community volunteer work all count. Write N/A if you have none.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Trainings, seminars, and workshops you attended, most recent first: the full title of the program, the dates, the number of hours, the type (managerial, supervisory, technical), and the complete name of the office or institution that ran it.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Your special skills and hobbies; any non-academic awards, recognitions or distinctions, written out in full; and any professional association you belong to, also written in full.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}These are the sworn questions — about relationships to people in the agency, administrative or criminal cases, convictions, separation from service, election candidacy, and indigenous or PWD status. Answer every one, and whenever you answer “Yes”, give the details in the space provided.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Three people who can vouch for you, and who are not related to you. Give each one’s complete name, their office or home address, and a contact number or email. Tell them you listed them.{% endblocktranslate %}

  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Sign the form — an e-signature is accepted. If you are unable to sign, use your right thumbmark instead.{% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Write your government ID number and the date it was issued.{% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Attach your passport-size photo (a digital photo is fine).{% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Put your signature and the date on the bottom of every page.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Finishing and uploading your PDS{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}This portal accepts PDF, JPG, and PNG files only — not Excel files. Choose one way:{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Option A — all on the computer{% endblocktranslate %}

  1. {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Add your e-signature (or digital certificate) and the date at the bottom of every page.{% endblocktranslate %}
  2. {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Add your digital passport-size photo in the photo box.{% endblocktranslate %}
  3. {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Save the finished file as PDF: in Excel, File → Save as (or Export) and choose PDF.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Option B — print and sign{% endblocktranslate %}

  1. {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Print the filled-up form.{% endblocktranslate %}
  2. {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Sign it and write the date at the bottom of every page, and attach your photo.{% endblocktranslate %}
  3. {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Scan it, or take a clear, straight photo of every page.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Either way:{% endblocktranslate %}

  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Include only the extra sheets you actually used. The workbook has continuation sheets (C5 to C11) for people who need more rows. If you did not use one, leave it out.{% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Check that your name, dates, and school names read exactly as they do on your other documents. Mismatches are the most common reason we return an application for correction.{% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %}Upload it on the application form under Personal Data Sheet, and the Work Experience Sheet under Work Experience Sheet.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}If a document is returned for correction, you will get an email that says what to fix and by when. You can re-upload from the same link — you do not start over.{% endblocktranslate %}
